# A Very Merry Navideño Quiz

A Christmas traditions quiz presentation for university students learning Spanish. The 11 questions are in English; answers and cultural explanations are revealed in a separate final round. Options are displayed for classroom discussion, with no on-screen selection or scoring.

Includes Christmas, New Year and Three Kings’ Day customs, photographs, labelled Wise Men illustrations, snowfall and festive decorations. The presentation scales with the screen height. Gentle original Christmas chimes start on interaction and can be muted with the sound button.

Open [the quiz on GitHub Pages](https://balpomorelitm.github.io/navidad-quiz/) or `index.html` in a browser. The HTML includes all images, styles, scripts and fonts and works offline.

Photograph credits, licences and research sources are available under **Sources & credits** in the answer round. Generated illustrations are identified there. Third-party photographs and fonts retain their respective licences.
