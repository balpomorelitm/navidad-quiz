# A Very Merry Navideño Quiz

A Christmas traditions quiz presentation for university students learning Spanish. The 12 questions are in English; answers and cultural explanations are revealed in a separate final round. Options are displayed for classroom discussion, with no on-screen selection or scoring.

Includes Christmas, New Year and Three Kings’ Day customs, a three-photo Night of the Radishes gallery, labelled Wise Men illustrations, snowfall and festive decorations. A multiple-answer Christmas carol question reveals three YouTube videos in the answer round. The Wise Men names remain the final question. The presentation scales with the screen height. Gentle original Christmas chimes start on interaction, can be muted and pause while a video is open.

Open [the quiz on GitHub Pages](https://balpomorelitm.github.io/navidad-quiz/) or `index.html` in a browser. The HTML includes all images, styles, scripts and fonts. Questions, explanations and photographs work offline; YouTube videos need an Internet connection and load only when you press **Play video**. Each player also includes a direct YouTube link.

Photograph credits, licences and research sources are available under **Sources & credits** in the answer round. Generated illustrations are identified there. Third-party photographs and fonts retain their respective licences.
