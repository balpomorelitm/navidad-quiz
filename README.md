# Spanish Quiz Night - Christmas Edition

[Open the presentation](https://balpomorelitm.github.io/navidad-quiz/)

A classroom presentation with 50 questions: Geography (5), History (5), Literature (10), Cinema & TV (10), Food & Drink (9), Christmas (11). The final three questions/options in every round are in Spanish except Food & Drink, which explicitly keeps all supplied PDF captures in their original English. All 19 Spanish solutions have italic English answer and explanation translations, shown only in the correction round.

Start with the Mingle Challenge (10 of 15 tasks, a different person each time, turron prize draw). Quiz questions come first, followed by sectioned answers, the winning-team announcement, an English guide to Spain's New Year chimes, the local official 2025-2026 Antena 3 excerpt, a prize draw, Christmas greetings and a final Instagram QR slide. There are 127 slides. Sport remains hidden until its materials arrive.

## Presenting

Click **Presenter** (or press **G**) to open the private control panel. It provides immediate access to the cover, every section, individual questions, solutions and the campanadas. Answers remain locked during the ordinary question sequence; the presenter can deliberately jump directly to an answer section.

Click **Open audience screen**, move that second window to the projector, and click **Activate screen** once to enable video and sound. Press **F** for full screen. The projected window omits private notes and navigation controls. Both windows must use the same browser and computer; state is synchronized through BroadcastChannel with local-storage fallback.

Use arrows, Page Up/Page Down, Enter/Space, or the panel buttons to advance. **Home** returns to the cover. Options appear A, B, C, D one second apart; the presenter can show all immediately. Options are static: no selections or scores are recorded.

**Presentation sound** contains independent music/effects volume controls (12% and 45% defaults). The supplied welcome MP3 loops only on the cover and Mingle slides; music stops for the quiz. Nine supplied local MP3 files provide the welcome loop, section bells, alternating question effects, a special cue on entering Spanish questions, the quiz start, answer transitions, a shared 6.5-second drumroll for the ticket and team winners, and winning-team celebration. Sound pauses during video and plays only in the audience window when connected. Enable sound once with a real click; preferences are saved and synchronized.

**Download campanadas video** is always available in the private presenter panel. Save the 18.4 MB MP4 before the event and open the downloaded file in the computer's video player for offline playback. The saved clip already starts at 1:14 of the original.

**Team winner** opens the announcement after the final answer key. Enter the winning team's name privately, then click **Announce winner**. 6.5 seconds of suspense with the supplied drumroll precede the large animated name, swinging background bells and celebration sound. The draft and pending name are not sent to the audience; only the revealed name is shared. Leaving during suspense cancels the announcement. Hide / reset conceals it; revisits and late audience connections do not replay the sound.

**Prize draw** opens an 6.5-second animation accompanied by the supplied drumroll. Set the eligible card count, then click **Start draw**. The final ticket is chosen from 1 to N using unbiased cryptographic randomness in modern browsers and shared with the audience. Generated almond-turron pieces fall continuously while the winner is displayed; reduced-motion mode shows a static scattering instead. The image uses one shared Blob URL, avoiding the browser CSS-variable size limit. Leaving during a draw cancels it; Reset prepares a new draw.

The cover borrows the supplied poster's illustrated style with real Lobster lettering. The HKU logo appears only there. New chapters have a brief pop transition. Christmas keeps snow, lights and garlands; other rounds feature animated globes, books, pages and film reels. Reduced-motion settings are respected. The final QR links to [@spanishprogramme_hku](https://www.instagram.com/spanishprogramme_hku/), verified from the programme's official website.

For local use, keep `index.html` and `media/` together. Main photographs, styles, scripts and fonts are embedded. The Food & Drink PDF page captures and separate option images load per slide from `media/gastronomia/`. Keep that folder with the presentation for offline use. The three cropped film clips, nine MP3 files and 78.8-second 720p campanadas excerpt work offline. Only YouTube carols require Internet. The new local video starts at exactly 1:14 of the supplied original. Its 18.42 MB H.264/AAC fast-start copy preserves 1280 x 720 resolution and ends before advertising. The local campanadas include the preamble, twelve chimes and New Year greeting; the old forced 70-second stop was removed. The official source link remains available.
