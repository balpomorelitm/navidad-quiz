# Navidad · A very merry Christmas quiz

An interactive Christmas traditions quiz for university students learning Spanish. The 11 questions are in English; answers and cultural explanations are revealed in a separate final round.

Includes Christmas, New Year and Three Kings’ Day customs, photographs, illustrations, snowfall and festive decorations.

Open `index.html` in a browser or use the GitHub Pages website. The HTML includes all images, styles, scripts and fonts and works offline.

Photograph credits, licences and research sources are available under **Sources & credits** in the answer round. Generated illustrations are identified there. Third-party photographs and fonts retain their respective licences.
